Each post sits in a different pay level under the 7th Pay Commission, belongs to a different department, and demands a different skill set.</p>

      <p>Understanding the difference between a PA and an LDC, for instance, can mean a <strong>₹10,000+ monthly salary gap with the same exam score</strong>. This guide covers every post in the SSC CHSL post list in full detail — pay, work profile, posting reality, salary breakdown, promotion pathways, and who should target what.</p>

    <section class="pc" id="ldc" aria-labelledby="ldc-h2">

      <div class="pc-head">

        <span class="pnum">POST 01</span>

        <h2 id="ldc-h2">Lower Division Clerk (LDC) — Pay Level 2</h2>

      </div>

      <div class="ptag">💰 Pay Level 2 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Basic Pay: ₹19,900/month</div>

      <p>The <strong>Lower Division Clerk (LDC)</strong> is the most widely available post in the SSC CHSL post list, with vacancies spread across more than 50 Central Government Ministries and Departments — from the Ministry of Finance to the Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Defence, Ministry of Railways, and Ministry of Agriculture. If you clear CHSL with a rank in the lower half of qualifiers, LDC is typically where allotment lands.</p>

 

      <h3>LDC Salary After 7th Pay Commission</h3>

      <p>LDC sits at <strong>Pay Level 2</strong> under the 7th Pay Commission, with a basic pay of ₹19,900 per month. After adding Dearness Allowance (DA at 50% of basic as of mid-2024), House Rent Allowance (HRA — 27% for X-cities, 18% for Y-cities, 9% for Z-cities), and Transport Allowance (TA), your monthly gross in-hand is approximately:</p>

      <ul class="ilist">

        <li><strong>X-category cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru):</strong> ₹29,000–₹32,000 per month</li>

        <li><strong>Y-category cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat):</strong> ₹27,000–₹30,000 per month</li>

        <li><strong>Z-category cities (smaller towns across India):</strong> ₹25,000–₹27,500 per month</li>

      </ul>

 

      <h3>LDC Work Profile and Daily Duties</h3>

      <p>An LDC handles routine clerical duties — maintaining government files and registers, drafting standard official correspondence, processing departmental applications, entering data into government portals, and assisting Section Officers with documentation. Working hours are fixed at <strong>9:30 AM to 6:00 PM</strong> with no rotational shifts. The work is procedural, stable, and predictable. For an aspirant from a Tier-2 city like Patna, Bhopal, or Coimbatore, an LDC posting in a Central Ministry offers long-term employment security that most entry-level private sector roles cannot replicate at the 12th-pass qualification level.</p>

 

      <h3>LDC Promotion Pathway</h3>

      <p>The promotion ladder for LDC is clearly defined and achievable through departmental exams: <strong>LDC (Pay Level 2) → Upper Division Clerk — UDC (Pay Level 4) → Assistant Section Officer — ASO (Pay Level 6, ₹35,400 basic) → Section Officer — SO (Pay Level 7)</strong>. Each step requires clearing a departmental competitive examination and typically takes 5–8 years per level depending on the Ministry and vacancy availability. Over a 20-year career, an LDC who clears all departmental exams can reach a Pay Level 7 salary of ₹44,900 basic — a significant long-term return from a 12th-pass entry point.</p>

Despite the modest starting salary, the job security, fixed 9:30–6 working hours, central government medical facilities (CGHS), 30 days of earned leave per year, and LTC benefits made the role far more stable than the private sector IT support jobs available in his city.     <section class="pc" id="jsa" aria-labelledby="jsa-h2">

      <div class="pc-head">

        <span class="pnum">POST 02</span>

        <h2 id="jsa-h2">Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) — Pay Level 2</h2>

      </div>

      <div class="ptag">💰 Pay Level 2 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Basic Pay: ₹19,900/month</div>

      <p>The <strong>Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)</strong> is functionally identical to LDC — same Pay Level 2, same basic pay of ₹19,900, same work nature, and the same promotion pathway. The only defining difference is the posting location. JSA positions are reserved exclusively for <strong>Parliament-attached offices</strong>: the Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, Ministry of External Affairs, and the Central Secretariat Service.</p>

 

      <h3>Why Students Prefer JSA Over LDC</h3>

      <p>Parliament-attached offices in New Delhi qualify for the maximum HRA slab (27% of basic in X-category cities), pushing gross in-hand slightly higher than a Ministry LDC posting in a Tier-2 city. But the more significant advantage is environmental. Parliament offices operate with stricter discipline, better office infrastructure, greater exposure to legislative and diplomatic processes, and a more professionally structured working culture. Many current government employees consistently report that JSA working conditions are meaningfully superior to routine Ministry LDC environments — without any difference in pay.</p>

      <div class="info">

        <strong>Important note on JSA vacancies:</strong> JSA openings are significantly fewer than LDC openings. In the <a href="https://ssc.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SSC CHSL 2024 notification</a>, JSA vacancies in Parliament offices numbered under 200, compared to thousands of LDC vacancies across general Ministries. A strong All India Rank — generally top 10–15% of qualifiers — is necessary to have a realistic shot at JSA allotment.

    <section class="pc" id="pa" aria-labelledby="pa-h2">

      <div class="pc-head">

        <span class="pnum">POST 03</span>

        <h2 id="pa-h2">Postal Assistant (PA) — Pay Level 4</h2>

      </div>

      <div class="ptag">💰 Pay Level 4 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Basic Pay: ₹25,500/month</div>

      <p>The <strong>Postal Assistant (PA)</strong> is consistently the most sought-after post in the SSC CHSL post list. It offers <strong>Pay Level 4 with a basic pay of ₹25,500</strong> — a ₹5,600 jump over LDC with the same exam score, same typing test requirement, and the same 12th-pass qualification. PA positions are filled under the <strong>Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications</strong>, with postings across head post offices, sub-offices, and postal divisions in every state and union territory.</p>

 

      <h3>PA Salary Breakdown — Gross In-Hand 2024</h3>

      <p>At Pay Level 4, the gross in-hand for a Postal Assistant in 2024 is significantly stronger than LDC across all city tiers:</p>

      <ul class="ilist">

        <li><strong>X-category metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata):</strong> ₹39,000–₹43,000 per month gross</li>

        <li><strong>Y-category cities (Lucknow, Jaipur, Pune, Bhopal, Patna):</strong> ₹36,000–₹40,000 per month gross</li>

        <li><strong>Z-category cities and smaller towns:</strong> ₹33,000–₹37,000 per month gross</li>

      </ul>

      <p>These figures include DA at current rates, city-appropriate HRA, and TA. In-hand salary increases each time DA is revised — typically twice a year in January and July based on the All India Consumer Price Index. This automatic increment mechanism means PA salaries rise steadily without requiring any action from the employee.</p>

 

      <h3>PA Work Profile — What You Actually Do Daily</h3>

      <p>A Postal Assistant works at post office counters and handles: <strong>Speed Post and Registered Post bookings and customer queries; Aadhaar-linked Post Office Savings Bank (POSB) account services; Postal Life Insurance (PLI) and Rural PLI premium collection; Money Order booking and payment; Parcel booking, tracking, and delivery coordination; Government bill payment services and e-services under India Post Payments Bank (IPPB)</strong>. The role is public-facing, dynamic, and computer-intensive. It requires accuracy with financial transactions, clear communication in Hindi and/or regional languages, and comfort with daily customer interaction.</p>

 

      <h3>PA Promotion Pathway — Inspector Posts and Beyond</h3>

      <p>The PA promotional track via the <strong>Inspector Posts (IP) examination</strong> is one of the best career growth paths among all SSC CHSL posts. The ladder runs: <strong>PA → Postal Supervisor → Inspector Posts (IP, Pay Level 7) → Assistant Superintendent Posts (ASP) → Superintendent Posts (SP, Group B Gazetted)</strong>. Reaching IP status — Group B Gazetted officer level — within 8–12 years of joining as a PA is achievable for diligent candidates. This is a significantly faster route to gazetted officer status than the LDC → ASO pathway.</p>

        <strong>Neha from Lucknow</strong> specifically targeted the PA post when preparing for SSC CHSL 2023, after using PrepGrind's salary calculator to compare all posts. Her gross salary at a Lucknow head post office came to ₹37,200 per month — nearly <strong>₹9,000 more per month than an LDC allotment at the same rank</strong>. Over a 25-year career with bi-annual DA revisions and Pay Commission increases, that starting difference compounds into lakhs of additional lifetime income.

      </div>

    <!-- POST 4: SA -->

    <section class="pc" id="sa" aria-labelledby="sa-h2">

      <div class="pc-head">

        <span class="pnum">POST 04</span>

        <h2 id="sa-h2">Sorting Assistant (SA) — Pay Level 4</h2>

      </div>

      <div class="ptag">💰 Pay Level 4 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Basic Pay: ₹25,500/month</div>

      <p>The <strong>Sorting Assistant (SA)</strong> is closely related to PA in terms of pay and department — same Department of Posts, same Pay Level 4, same basic pay of ₹25,500, and the same salary structure. The difference lies in the work environment, day-to-day duties, and the shift schedule, which is a significant practical consideration before selecting SA as your preferred post.</p>

 

      <h3>SA Work Profile — Backend Postal Operations</h3>

      <p>A Sorting Assistant works at <strong>Mail Business Centres (MBC), Parcel Hubs, and Speed Post Sorting Hubs</strong> — not at customer-facing post office counters. Daily work involves: physically sorting incoming and outgoing postal articles by zone and pin code; managing dispatch registers and manifests; operating and monitoring automated mail sorting machines in larger hubs; verifying addresses and redirecting mis-sorted items; and coordinating outgoing dispatch to delivery postmen and delivery hubs. The role is operationally focused, less interpersonal, and more logistics-oriented than PA counter work.</p>

 

      <h3>Shift Duties — The Most Important Factor in PA vs SA Decision</h3>

      <p>The single most consequential difference between PA and SA is shift duty. SA roles at major sorting hubs in Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, and Bengaluru typically operate on <strong>three rotational shifts: morning (6 AM–2 PM), evening (2 PM–10 PM), and night (10 PM–6 AM)</strong>. This rotational shift system is mandatory and non-negotiable in most major hub postings. If you strongly prefer fixed daytime working hours, PA is the better choice at the same pay. If you are comfortable with shifts — or actively prefer the shift allowance that comes with night duty — SA is equally valid financially.</p>

 

      <div class="warn">

        <strong>⚠️ Consider this before choosing SA:</strong> Many aspirants from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities specifically request SA postings at their home state's sorting centre — Bhopal, Patna, Ranchi, Thiruvananthapuram — to minimise relocation costs and stay near family. SA posting requests to home-state circles are often more accommodatable than PA counter postings, which are subject to specific post office vacancy availability. If staying geographically close to home matters more than shift schedule, SA can actually work in your favour.

      </div>

    </section>

 

    <!-- POST 5: DEO -->

    <section class="pc" id="deo" aria-labelledby="deo-h2">

      <div class="pc-head">

        <span class="pnum">POST 05</span>

        <h2 id="deo-h2">Data Entry Operator (DEO) — Pay Level 4 &amp; 5</h2>

      </div>

      <div class="ptag">💰 Pay Level 4–5 &nbsp;|&nbsp; Basic Pay: ₹25,500–₹29,200/month</div>

      <p>The <strong>Data Entry Operator (DEO)</strong> is the most technically demanding post in the SSC CHSL post list — and the most financially rewarding if you can clear the Skill Test. DEO is available in two grades with different pay levels and different posting offices:</p>

      <ul class="ilist">

        <li><strong>DEO Grade A — Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic):</strong> Posted in various Central Government Ministries including Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Ministry of Labour and Employment, Ministry of Commerce, and other data-processing-heavy departments.</li>

        <li><strong>DEO Grade B (CAG) — Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic):</strong> Posted in the <strong>Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG)</strong>. This is the <strong>highest-paying post in the entire SSC CHSL post list</strong> — ₹9,300 more basic pay than LDC from Day 1.</li>

      </ul>

      <p>At Pay Level 5, the DEO Grade B at CAG gross in-hand in metro cities ranges between <strong>₹42,000 and ₹46,000 per month</strong> — significantly ahead of every other CHSL post including PA and SA.</p>

 

      <h3>DEO Skill Test — 8,000 KDPH Explained</h3>

      <p>The DEO Skill Test requires completing a <strong>data entry passage at 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)</strong>. This is not a conventional typing test of running text — it involves structured data formats including numbers, dates, symbols, abbreviations, and mixed content that appear in actual government data entry workflows. The test duration is typically 15 minutes with a standardised passage. You must maintain 8,000 KDPH across the full passage, and accuracy errors reduce your effective KDPH score. A large percentage of candidates who clear the written exam fail the skill test because they practice general typing rather than government-format data entry.</p>

 

      <h3>DEO Work Profile — Daily Job at Central Ministries and CAG</h3>

      <p>DEOs process and enter financial records, government census and survey data, audit worksheets, and structured administrative datasets into departmental software systems. At CAG specifically, the work involves entering audit observations, financial reports, and account statements. The work is highly computer-intensive, detail-oriented, and requires sustained accuracy over full working days. It suits candidates with strong computer literacy, numerical precision, and a preference for analytical desk work over customer interaction or physical logistics.</p>

 

      <div class="warn">

        <strong>⚠️ Honest preparation caveat:</strong> If your current typing speed is below 6,000 KDPH, you need a minimum of 3–4 months of daily 30–45 minute practice on government-format data entry simulators — not general typing websites — to reach 8,000 KDPH reliably under exam conditions. The format difference between casual typing and government data entry passages is significant. Budget adequate preparation time before banking on DEO as your target post.

      </div>

The CAG posting offers Pay Level 5 — the highest in CHSL — stable work at a constitutional body, strong departmental benefits, and a clear promotional path towards Assistant Audit Officer (Pay Level 8) over a 12–15 year career. See our <a href="https://prepgrind.com/ssc-chsl-deo-preparation" title="DEO skill test preparation guide">DEO skill test preparation guide on PrepGrind</a> for a structured 3-month practice plan.

      <div class="fi" id="f1">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f1')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f1b">

          <span>What is the difference between PA and SA in SSC CHSL?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f1b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">Both PA (Postal Assistant) and SA (Sorting Assistant) are Pay Level 4 posts under the Department of Posts with identical basic pay of ₹25,500. PA works at post office counters in a customer-facing role handling Speed Post, POSB account services, money orders, and PLI collections. SA works at Mail Sorting Hubs in a backend logistics role sorting and dispatching postal articles. The key practical difference: SA roles frequently involve rotational night shift duties at major hubs, while PA roles have fixed daytime hours. Choose PA if you prefer customer interaction and fixed hours; SA if you prefer independent operational work and can manage shifts. Read our detailed <a href="https://prepgrind.com/ssc-chsl-pa-vs-sa" title="PA vs SA comparison guide">PA vs SA comparison guide</a> for a full breakdown.</div>

        </div>

      </div>

 

      <div class="fi" id="f2">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f2')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f2b">

          <span>Is DEO a better post than LDC in SSC CHSL?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f2b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">Financially, DEO is significantly better. DEO sits at Pay Level 4 or 5 versus Pay Level 2 for LDC — meaning ₹5,600 to ₹9,300 higher basic pay from Day 1. DEO Grade B at CAG is the highest-paying post in the entire SSC CHSL post list at Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic). However, DEO requires clearing an 8,000 KDPH data entry skill test which many candidates fail even after clearing the written exam. If you have strong typing accuracy and build up to 8,000 KDPH in preparation, DEO CAG is clearly the superior post. If your typing speed is average, LDC gives you stable government employment without the skill test risk.</div>

        </div>

      </div>

 

      <div class="fi" id="f3">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f3')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f3b">

          <span>Can I choose my preferred post in SSC CHSL?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f3b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">Yes, SSC CHSL applicants can fill in post preferences in the application form, and you should do so thoughtfully. However, final allotment is strictly based on your All India Rank and the vacancies available in each post category at the time of allotment. Listing PA as preference #1 does not guarantee a PA posting — your rank must be strong enough that PA vacancies remain available when your name is reached in the merit list. This is the single most important reason to aim for a competitive score, not just cutoff clearance. Use PrepGrind's <a href="https://prepgrind.com/ssc-chsl-cut-off" title="SSC CHSL cutoff trends">CHSL cutoff tool</a> to estimate the score needed for your preferred post.</div>

        </div>

      </div>

 

      <div class="fi" id="f4">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f4')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f4b">

          <span>What is the JSA post in SSC CHSL, and is it worth targeting over LDC?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f4b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant) carries Pay Level 2 with ₹19,900 basic pay — identical to LDC. The difference is that JSA is posted exclusively in Parliament-attached offices: Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, and Ministry of External Affairs. The work environment, office infrastructure, and professional exposure in these offices are meaningfully superior to routine Ministry LDC work. Most exam experts and PrepGrind mentors recommend preferring JSA over a general LDC posting if your rank allows it. The tradeoff: JSA almost always means a permanent Delhi posting, which may not suit every aspirant's family or financial situation.</div>

        </div>

      </div>

 

      <div class="fi" id="f5">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f5')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f5b">

          <span>What typing speed is required for SSC CHSL posts?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f5b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">For LDC, JSA, PA, and SA posts, the SSC CHSL Typing Test requires <strong>35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi</strong>. For DEO posts, the requirement is <strong>8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH)</strong> in a data entry skill test — stricter and more accuracy-dependent than regular typing. Start typing practice at least 2–3 months before your expected skill test date. For DEO, aim for 8,500 KDPH in practice to maintain a safety buffer. Use government-format data entry simulators for DEO preparation, not general typing websites — the passage content and format differ significantly.</div>

        </div>

      </div>

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f6')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f6b">

          <span>What is the SSC CHSL salary after 7th Pay Commission for all posts?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f6b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">According to the <a href="https://finmin.nic.in" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">7th Pay Commission Pay Matrix (finmin.nic.in)</a>, SSC CHSL salaries in 2024 are: LDC and JSA — basic ₹19,900 (Pay Level 2), gross ₹25,000–₹34,000 depending on city. PA and SA — basic ₹25,500 (Pay Level 4), gross ₹33,000–₹43,000. DEO Grade A — basic ₹25,500 (Pay Level 4), gross ₹33,000–₹42,000. DEO Grade B/CAG — basic ₹29,200 (Pay Level 5), gross ₹38,000–₹46,000. All gross figures include current DA, HRA by city tier, and TA. NPS deduction of approximately 10% of basic + DA applies additionally. See our complete <a href="https://prepgrind.com/ssc-chsl-salary" title="SSC CHSL salary in hand breakdown 2024">SSC CHSL salary in-hand breakdown guide</a> for post-wise calculations.</div>

      </div>

 

      <div class="fi" id="f7">

        <button class="fb2" onclick="tf('f7')" aria-expanded="false" aria-controls="f7b">

          <span>Which SSC CHSL post has the best promotion opportunities?</span>

          <span class="fic" aria-hidden="true">+</span>

        </button>

        <div class="fbody" id="f7b" role="region">

          <div class="finn">PA and SA offer one of the strongest promotion structures in the SSC CHSL post list. The Inspector Posts (IP) exam allows PA/SA candidates to reach Group B Gazetted status within 8–12 years — faster than most other SSC routes. LDC and JSA promote to ASO (Pay Level 6) and Section Officer (Pay Level 7) through departmental LDCE exams, typically over 10–15 years. DEO at CAG has a unique track reaching Assistant Audit Officer (Pay Level 8) — one of the highest pay levels reachable from any SSC CHSL entry point. Your ideal post also depends on which promotional exam you are most willing to prepare for post-joining.</div>
