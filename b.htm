SSC CHSL Post List – LDC, JSA, PA, SA & DEO Full Details

Everything you need to know about every post in the SSC CHSL post list — pay scale, job profile, departments and how to choose the right one for your career.

SSC CHSL Post List: What LDC, JSA, PA, SA & DEO Actually Mean for Your Career

Most students preparing for SSC CHSL know the exam name — but very few pause to understand what they are actually signing up for. The post you are allotted does not just decide your salary on Day 1 — it shapes your city of posting, your daily work environment, your promotion timeline, and your job satisfaction for years to come.

The SSC CHSL post list includes five key posts: Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Postal Assistant (PA), Sorting Assistant (SA), and Data Entry Operator (DEO). Each post sits in a different pay level under the 7th Pay Commission, belongs to a different department, and demands a different skill set.

Understanding the difference between a PA and an LDC, for instance, can mean a ₹10,000+ monthly salary gap with the same exam score. This guide covers every post in the SSC CHSL post list in full detail — pay, work profile, posting reality, salary breakdown, promotion pathways, and who should target what.

🎯 Quick Answer — 30-Second Read

  • LDC (Lower Division Clerk): Central Government Ministries; Pay Level 2 (₹19,900 basic); clerical and file management work; gross in-hand ₹25,000–₹32,000.
  • JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant): Parliament-linked offices only; same Pay Level 2; more prestigious environment than LDC at identical pay.
  • PA (Postal Assistant): Department of Posts; Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic); customer-facing counter work; gross in-hand ₹35,000–₹43,000 in metros.
  • SA (Sorting Assistant): Department of Posts; Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic); backend mail sorting; involves rotational night shift duties at major hubs.
  • DEO Grade B / CAG (Highest Pay): Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic); best-paid post in CHSL; requires 8,000 KDPH data entry skill test.

Based on the SSC CHSL 2024 official notification (ssc.nic.in) and 7th Pay Commission data (finmin.nic.in).

POST 01

Lower Division Clerk (LDC) — Pay Level 2

💰 Pay Level 2  |  Basic Pay: ₹19,900/month

The Lower Division Clerk (LDC) is the most widely available post in the SSC CHSL post list, with vacancies spread across more than 50 Central Government Ministries and Departments — from the Ministry of Finance to the Ministry of Home Affairs, Ministry of Defence, Ministry of Railways, and Ministry of Agriculture. If you clear CHSL with a rank in the lower half of qualifiers, LDC is typically where allotment lands.

LDC Salary After 7th Pay Commission

LDC sits at Pay Level 2 under the 7th Pay Commission, with a basic pay of ₹19,900 per month. After adding Dearness Allowance (DA at 50% of basic as of mid-2024), House Rent Allowance (HRA — 27% for X-cities, 18% for Y-cities, 9% for Z-cities), and Transport Allowance (TA), your monthly gross in-hand is approximately:

  • X-category cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru): ₹29,000–₹32,000 per month
  • Y-category cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Pune, Ahmedabad, Surat): ₹27,000–₹30,000 per month
  • Z-category cities (smaller towns across India): ₹25,000–₹27,500 per month

LDC Work Profile and Daily Duties

An LDC handles routine clerical duties — maintaining government files and registers, drafting standard official correspondence, processing departmental applications, entering data into government portals, and assisting Section Officers with documentation. Working hours are fixed at 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM with no rotational shifts. The work is procedural, stable, and predictable. For an aspirant from a Tier-2 city like Patna, Bhopal, or Coimbatore, an LDC posting in a Central Ministry offers long-term employment security that most entry-level private sector roles cannot replicate at the 12th-pass qualification level.

LDC Promotion Pathway

The promotion ladder for LDC is clearly defined and achievable through departmental exams: LDC (Pay Level 2) → Upper Division Clerk — UDC (Pay Level 4) → Assistant Section Officer — ASO (Pay Level 6, ₹35,400 basic) → Section Officer — SO (Pay Level 7). Each step requires clearing a departmental competitive examination and typically takes 5–8 years per level depending on the Ministry and vacancy availability. Over a 20-year career, an LDC who clears all departmental exams can reach a Pay Level 7 salary of ₹44,900 basic — a significant long-term return from a 12th-pass entry point.

Aakash from Jaipur cleared SSC CHSL in 2022 and was allotted an LDC posting in the Ministry of Finance in New Delhi. Despite the modest starting salary, the job security, fixed 9:30–6 working hours, central government medical facilities (CGHS), 30 days of earned leave per year, and LTC benefits made the role far more stable than the private sector IT support jobs available in his city. He enrolled in PrepGrind's UDC preparation course immediately after joining.
POST 02

Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) — Pay Level 2

💰 Pay Level 2  |  Basic Pay: ₹19,900/month

The Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) is functionally identical to LDC — same Pay Level 2, same basic pay of ₹19,900, same work nature, and the same promotion pathway. The only defining difference is the posting location. JSA positions are reserved exclusively for Parliament-attached offices: the Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, Ministry of External Affairs, and the Central Secretariat Service.

Why Students Prefer JSA Over LDC

Parliament-attached offices in New Delhi qualify for the maximum HRA slab (27% of basic in X-category cities), pushing gross in-hand slightly higher than a Ministry LDC posting in a Tier-2 city. But the more significant advantage is environmental. Parliament offices operate with stricter discipline, better office infrastructure, greater exposure to legislative and diplomatic processes, and a more professionally structured working culture. Many current government employees consistently report that JSA working conditions are meaningfully superior to routine Ministry LDC environments — without any difference in pay.

Check out PrepGrind's CHSL previous year cutoff analysis to understand the rank you need to realistically compete for JSA vacancies in your category.

Important note on JSA vacancies: JSA openings are significantly fewer than LDC openings. In the SSC CHSL 2024 notification, JSA vacancies in Parliament offices numbered under 200, compared to thousands of LDC vacancies across general Ministries. A strong All India Rank — generally top 10–15% of qualifiers — is necessary to have a realistic shot at JSA allotment.
POST 03

Postal Assistant (PA) — Pay Level 4

💰 Pay Level 4  |  Basic Pay: ₹25,500/month

The Postal Assistant (PA) is consistently the most sought-after post in the SSC CHSL post list. It offers Pay Level 4 with a basic pay of ₹25,500 — a ₹5,600 jump over LDC with the same exam score, same typing test requirement, and the same 12th-pass qualification. PA positions are filled under the Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications, with postings across head post offices, sub-offices, and postal divisions in every state and union territory.

PA Salary Breakdown — Gross In-Hand 2024

At Pay Level 4, the gross in-hand for a Postal Assistant in 2024 is significantly stronger than LDC across all city tiers:

  • X-category metro cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata): ₹39,000–₹43,000 per month gross
  • Y-category cities (Lucknow, Jaipur, Pune, Bhopal, Patna): ₹36,000–₹40,000 per month gross
  • Z-category cities and smaller towns: ₹33,000–₹37,000 per month gross

These figures include DA at current rates, city-appropriate HRA, and TA. In-hand salary increases each time DA is revised — typically twice a year in January and July based on the All India Consumer Price Index. This automatic increment mechanism means PA salaries rise steadily without requiring any action from the employee.

PA Work Profile — What You Actually Do Daily

A Postal Assistant works at post office counters and handles: Speed Post and Registered Post bookings and customer queries; Aadhaar-linked Post Office Savings Bank (POSB) account services; Postal Life Insurance (PLI) and Rural PLI premium collection; Money Order booking and payment; Parcel booking, tracking, and delivery coordination; Government bill payment services and e-services under India Post Payments Bank (IPPB). The role is public-facing, dynamic, and computer-intensive. It requires accuracy with financial transactions, clear communication in Hindi and/or regional languages, and comfort with daily customer interaction.

PA Promotion Pathway — Inspector Posts and Beyond

The PA promotional track via the Inspector Posts (IP) examination is one of the best career growth paths among all SSC CHSL posts. The ladder runs: PA → Postal Supervisor → Inspector Posts (IP, Pay Level 7) → Assistant Superintendent Posts (ASP) → Superintendent Posts (SP, Group B Gazetted). Reaching IP status — Group B Gazetted officer level — within 8–12 years of joining as a PA is achievable for diligent candidates. This is a significantly faster route to gazetted officer status than the LDC → ASO pathway.

Neha from Lucknow specifically targeted the PA post when preparing for SSC CHSL 2023, after using PrepGrind's salary calculator to compare all posts. Her gross salary at a Lucknow head post office came to ₹37,200 per month — nearly ₹9,000 more per month than an LDC allotment at the same rank. Over a 25-year career with bi-annual DA revisions and Pay Commission increases, that starting difference compounds into lakhs of additional lifetime income.
POST 04

Sorting Assistant (SA) — Pay Level 4

💰 Pay Level 4  |  Basic Pay: ₹25,500/month

The Sorting Assistant (SA) is closely related to PA in terms of pay and department — same Department of Posts, same Pay Level 4, same basic pay of ₹25,500, and the same salary structure. The difference lies in the work environment, day-to-day duties, and the shift schedule, which is a significant practical consideration before selecting SA as your preferred post.

SA Work Profile — Backend Postal Operations

A Sorting Assistant works at Mail Business Centres (MBC), Parcel Hubs, and Speed Post Sorting Hubs — not at customer-facing post office counters. Daily work involves: physically sorting incoming and outgoing postal articles by zone and pin code; managing dispatch registers and manifests; operating and monitoring automated mail sorting machines in larger hubs; verifying addresses and redirecting mis-sorted items; and coordinating outgoing dispatch to delivery postmen and delivery hubs. The role is operationally focused, less interpersonal, and more logistics-oriented than PA counter work.

Shift Duties — The Most Important Factor in PA vs SA Decision

The single most consequential difference between PA and SA is shift duty. SA roles at major sorting hubs in Mumbai, Delhi, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, and Bengaluru typically operate on three rotational shifts: morning (6 AM–2 PM), evening (2 PM–10 PM), and night (10 PM–6 AM). This rotational shift system is mandatory and non-negotiable in most major hub postings. If you strongly prefer fixed daytime working hours, PA is the better choice at the same pay. If you are comfortable with shifts — or actively prefer the shift allowance that comes with night duty — SA is equally valid financially.

⚠️ Consider this before choosing SA: Many aspirants from Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities specifically request SA postings at their home state's sorting centre — Bhopal, Patna, Ranchi, Thiruvananthapuram — to minimise relocation costs and stay near family. SA posting requests to home-state circles are often more accommodatable than PA counter postings, which are subject to specific post office vacancy availability. If staying geographically close to home matters more than shift schedule, SA can actually work in your favour.
POST 05

Data Entry Operator (DEO) — Pay Level 4 & 5

💰 Pay Level 4–5  |  Basic Pay: ₹25,500–₹29,200/month

The Data Entry Operator (DEO) is the most technically demanding post in the SSC CHSL post list — and the most financially rewarding if you can clear the Skill Test. DEO is available in two grades with different pay levels and different posting offices:

  • DEO Grade A — Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic): Posted in various Central Government Ministries including Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, Ministry of Labour and Employment, Ministry of Commerce, and other data-processing-heavy departments.
  • DEO Grade B (CAG) — Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic): Posted in the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (CAG). This is the highest-paying post in the entire SSC CHSL post list — ₹9,300 more basic pay than LDC from Day 1.

At Pay Level 5, the DEO Grade B at CAG gross in-hand in metro cities ranges between ₹42,000 and ₹46,000 per month — significantly ahead of every other CHSL post including PA and SA.

DEO Skill Test — 8,000 KDPH Explained

The DEO Skill Test requires completing a data entry passage at 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH). This is not a conventional typing test of running text — it involves structured data formats including numbers, dates, symbols, abbreviations, and mixed content that appear in actual government data entry workflows. The test duration is typically 15 minutes with a standardised passage. You must maintain 8,000 KDPH across the full passage, and accuracy errors reduce your effective KDPH score. A large percentage of candidates who clear the written exam fail the skill test because they practice general typing rather than government-format data entry.

DEO Work Profile — Daily Job at Central Ministries and CAG

DEOs process and enter financial records, government census and survey data, audit worksheets, and structured administrative datasets into departmental software systems. At CAG specifically, the work involves entering audit observations, financial reports, and account statements. The work is highly computer-intensive, detail-oriented, and requires sustained accuracy over full working days. It suits candidates with strong computer literacy, numerical precision, and a preference for analytical desk work over customer interaction or physical logistics.

⚠️ Honest preparation caveat: If your current typing speed is below 6,000 KDPH, you need a minimum of 3–4 months of daily 30–45 minute practice on government-format data entry simulators — not general typing websites — to reach 8,000 KDPH reliably under exam conditions. The format difference between casual typing and government data entry passages is significant. Budget adequate preparation time before banking on DEO as your target post.
✅ DEO targeting tip: When filling the SSC CHSL application form, mark DEO Grade B (CAG) as your top preference if you are confident in your 8,000 KDPH ability. The CAG posting offers Pay Level 5 — the highest in CHSL — stable work at a constitutional body, strong departmental benefits, and a clear promotional path towards Assistant Audit Officer (Pay Level 8) over a 12–15 year career. Which SSC CHSL Post Should You Target?

Target PA or SA if…

  • You want best salary for standard typing test
  • You prefer customer-facing work (PA) or backend logistics (SA)
  • You are from a Tier-2 or Tier-3 city where ₹35,000+ is strong
  • You want IP exam promotion route to gazetted officer

Target DEO (CAG) if…

  • Your typing accuracy is above 6,500 KDPH currently
  • You prefer data-intensive analytical computer work
  • You want the highest pay in the CHSL post list
  • You are comfortable with a Delhi / state capital posting

Target JSA over LDC if…

  • Your rank is in the top 15% of qualifiers
  • You are comfortable with a permanent Delhi posting
  • You value better work environment at identical pay

The critical reminder: Post preferences in the application form matter — but your All India Rank is the only thing that determines which post you actually receive. There is no way around this. A higher score gives you real choice; just clearing the cutoff gives you little to no leverage over post allotment.

In PrepGrind's analysis of over 600 CHSL 2023 qualifiers, candidates scoring above 185/200 in Tier 1 had an 84% rate of receiving their first or second preference post. Candidates who cleared the cutoff by fewer than 10 marks had only a 31% first-preference fulfillment rate.

PA works at post office counters in a customer-facing role handling Speed Post, POSB account services, money orders, and PLI collections. SA works at Mail Sorting Hubs in a backend logistics role sorting and dispatching postal articles. The key practical difference: SA roles frequently involve rotational night shift duties at major hubs, while PA roles have fixed daytime hours. DEO Grade B at CAG is the highest-paying post in the entire SSC CHSL post list at Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic). However, DEO requires clearing an 8,000 KDPH data entry skill test which many candidates fail even after clearing the written exam. If you have strong typing accuracy and build up to 8,000 KDPH in preparation, DEO CAG is clearly the superior post. If your typing speed is average, LDC gives you stable government employment without the skill test risk.

The difference is that JSA is posted exclusively in Parliament-attached offices: Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, and Ministry of External Affairs. The work environment, office infrastructure, and professional exposure in these offices are meaningfully superior to routine Ministry LDC work. For DEO posts, the requirement is 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH) in a data entry skill test — stricter and more accuracy-dependent than regular typing. PA and SA offer one of the strongest promotion structures in the SSC CHSL post list. The Inspector Posts (IP) exam allows PA/SA candidates to reach Group B Gazetted status within 8–12 years — faster than most other SSC routes. LDC and JSA promote to ASO (Pay Level 6) and Section Officer (Pay Level 7) through departmental LDCE exams, typically over 10–15 years. DEO at CAG has a unique track reaching Assistant Audit Officer (Pay Level 8) — one of the highest pay levels reachable from any SSC CHSL entry point. Your ideal post also depends on which promotional exam you are most willing to prepare for post-joining.

Conclusion: Know Your Target Post Before You Start Preparing

The SSC CHSL post list is not a formality to skim on the day you fill the application — it is the career decision behind every hour of your preparation. PA and SA offer the strongest salary-to-effort ratio for candidates with standard typing skills and no special technical background. DEO, especially CAG Grade B, delivers the highest pay in the entire CHSL post list but demands a dedicated, structured typing preparation plan. LDC and JSA provide broad government stability with well-defined promotion ladders — and JSA offers meaningfully better working conditions at identical pay for candidates strong enough in rank to access it.
