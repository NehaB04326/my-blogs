SSC CHSL Post List: What LDC, JSA, PA, SA & DEO Actually Mean for Your Career

Most students preparing for SSC CHSL know the exam name — but very few pause to understand what they're actually signing up for. The post you're allotted doesn't just decide your salary on Day 1 — it shapes your city of posting, your daily work environment, your promotion timeline, and honestly, how satisfied you'll feel five years into the job.

This article breaks down every post in the SSC CHSL post list — LDC, JSA, PA, SA, and DEO — with pay scales, ministries, work profiles, and what a typical workday actually looks like. No generic overviews. Just the specific, verified details you need to decide which post to prioritise in your application form.

Understanding the difference between a PA and an LDC, for instance, can mean a ₹10,000+ monthly salary difference — with the same exam score. Knowing this before you fill out your preferences is the kind of edge most aspirants miss entirely.

🎯 Quick Answer — 30-Second Read

  • LDC (Lower Division Clerk): Posted across Central Government Ministries; Pay Level 2 (₹19,900 basic); handles clerical and file management work.
  • JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant): Same role and pay as LDC but posted specifically in Parliament-linked offices — considered more prestigious.
  • PA (Postal Assistant): Under Department of Posts; Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic); customer-facing counter work at post offices.
  • SA (Sorting Assistant): Also under Department of Posts at Pay Level 4; handles backend mail sorting and dispatch — often involves shift duties.
  • DEO (Data Entry Operator): Pay Level 4 or 5 depending on the office; requires 8,000 KDPH typing speed; most technically demanding post in CHSL.

Based on the official SSC CHSL 2024 notification and 7th Pay Commission data published on ssc.nic.in.

POST 01

Lower Division Clerk (LDC)

💰 Pay Level 2  |  ₹19,900 Basic

The LDC is the most widely available post in the SSC CHSL post list, with vacancies spread across dozens of Central Government Ministries and Departments — from the Ministry of Finance to the Ministry of Defence. If you clear CHSL and don't have a strong rank, LDC is often where you land, which makes it important to understand clearly.

Pay and salary: LDC sits at Pay Level 2 under the 7th Pay Commission, with a basic pay of ₹19,900 per month. After adding Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA based on city), and Transport Allowance (TA), your gross in-hand typically ranges between ₹25,000 and ₹32,000 per month depending on your posting city.

Work profile: An LDC handles routine clerical duties — maintaining files, drafting standard correspondence, updating government registers, processing applications, and data entry. The work is largely procedural and desk-based. Working hours are typically 9:30 AM to 6:00 PM with no shift duties.

Promotion pathway: Through departmental exams, an LDC can be promoted to Upper Division Clerk (UDC) and eventually to Assistant Section Officer (ASO), which sits at Pay Level 6 (₹35,400 basic) — a significant long-term growth path.

Aakash from Jaipur, who cleared SSC CHSL in 2022, was allotted an LDC post in the Ministry of Finance. Despite the modest starting salary, the job security, predictable working hours, and access to central government benefits made it far more stable than the private sector options available in his city.
POST 02

Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)

💰 Pay Level 2  |  ₹19,900 Basic

JSA is functionally identical to LDC — same pay grade, same work nature, same promotion pathway. The single defining difference is where you're posted. JSA positions are reserved specifically for the Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, Ministry of External Affairs, and a few other Parliament-attached bodies.

Pay and salary: JSA also sits at Pay Level 2, so the base pay is identical to LDC at ₹19,900. However, Parliament-attached offices in New Delhi attract the maximum HRA (27% of basic in X-category cities), pushing your gross in-hand toward the higher end of the ₹28,000–₹34,000 range.

Why students prefer JSA over LDC: The working environment in Parliament offices tends to be more structured, better resourced, and carries a certain professional prestige. Exposure to legislative and diplomatic processes gives JSA postings a distinct character compared to routine ministerial LDC work. If your rank is strong enough to have a choice, many experienced PrepGrind mentors recommend preferring JSA over a general Ministry LDC posting.

POST 03

Postal Assistant (PA)

💰 Pay Level 4  |  ₹25,500 Basic

PA is one of the two most sought-after posts in the SSC CHSL post list, and for good reason — it offers Pay Level 4 with a substantial salary jump over LDC. PA positions are filled under the Department of Posts, Ministry of Communications, and postings happen across head post offices, sub-offices, and sorting divisions nationwide.

Pay and salary: At Pay Level 4, the gross in-hand for a PA ranges between ₹35,000 and ₹43,000 per month in metro and Tier-1 cities after DA, HRA, and TA. In smaller cities, it may be slightly lower due to reduced HRA slabs, but the basic pay remains the same.

Work profile: A Postal Assistant works at post office counters — handling Speed Post and Registered Post bookings, operating Aadhaar-linked savings and insurance services, managing money orders, and assisting customers with postal queries. The role is public-facing and requires basic computer proficiency and clear communication.

Promotion pathway: PA can be promoted to Postal Service Group B through departmental exams, with significant long-term growth within the postal department.

Neha from Lucknow specifically targeted the PA post in SSC CHSL 2023, having compared pay scales carefully beforehand. Her gross salary at a Lucknow head post office came to ₹37,200 per month — nearly ₹9,000 more than what she'd have earned as an LDC at the same rank.
POST 04

Sorting Assistant (SA)

💰 Pay Level 4  |  ₹25,500 Basic

SA is closely related to PA — same department (Department of Posts), same Pay Level 4, and same salary structure. The distinction is entirely in the work environment and daily duties.

Work profile: A Sorting Assistant works at Mail Business Centres and Speed Post Sorting Hubs rather than at customer-facing counters. The job involves physically sorting incoming and outgoing mail, managing dispatch registers, operating sorting machines, and ensuring accurate distribution of postal articles across zones. It's more operational and logistics-oriented compared to the PA role.

Shift duties — key consideration: SA roles frequently involve rotational shift duties including evening and night shifts, particularly in high-volume sorting hubs in cities like Mumbai, Delhi, and Chennai. If you prefer fixed working hours, this is an important factor before selecting SA as your preferred post.

Who should target SA: If you prefer working independently without constant customer interaction, and you're comfortable with shift-based schedules, SA is a solid choice at the same pay as PA. Many aspirants from smaller towns request SA postings in their home state's sorting centres to stay geographically close to family.

POST 05

Data Entry Operator (DEO)

💰 Pay Level 4–5  |  ₹25,500–₹29,200 Basic

DEO is the most technically demanding post in the entire SSC CHSL framework — and also the most rewarding financially if you can clear the skill test. There are two grades:

  • DEO Grade A: Pay Level 4 (₹25,500 basic) — posted in various Central Government Ministries.
  • DEO Grade B (CAG): Pay Level 5 (₹29,200 basic) — posted in the Office of the Comptroller & Auditor General of India. This is the highest-paying post in the SSC CHSL post list.

Skill test requirement: DEO candidates must clear a Data Entry Skill Test requiring 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH). This is stricter than the standard typing test for LDC/PA/SA (35 WPM in English or 30 WPM in Hindi). The 8,000 KDPH standard measures not just speed but accuracy on a formatted data entry passage — a large number of aspirants fail here despite clearing the written exam.

Work profile: DEOs process financial records, enter audit and census data, manage government databases, and handle structured data processing tasks. It suits students with strong computer backgrounds and a preference for analytical desk work.

⚠️ Honest caveat: If your typing speed is below 6,000 KDPH currently, you'll need consistent daily practice for at least 3–4 months to reach 8,000 KDPH reliably under exam conditions. Start early — don't underestimate the skill test.

Which SSC CHSL Post Should You Target?

Target PA or SA if…

  • You want the highest salary for a standard typing test
  • You're from a Tier-2 or Tier-3 city where ₹35,000+ is strong
  • PA = you like people-facing work; SA = you prefer backend ops

Target DEO (CAG) if…

  • Your typing accuracy is above 6,500 KDPH currently
  • You prefer data-intensive computer work
  • You want the highest possible pay grade within CHSL

Target JSA over LDC if…

  • Your rank is strong enough for Parliament office posting
  • You want better work environment at the same pay
  • You're okay with a Delhi-based posting long-term

Important reminder: Post allotment is merit-based and vacancy-dependent. You can indicate preferences, but a higher score is the only reliable way to secure your preferred post.

In the SSC CHSL 2023 cycle, according to PrepGrind's analysis of student outcomes, candidates who scored above 185/200 in Tier 1 had a significantly higher rate of PA and DEO allotments compared to those who cleared the cutoff by a narrow margin.

Fill your preferences carefully in the application form — and focus on maximising your Tier 1 and Tier 2 scores first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Both PA (Postal Assistant) and SA (Sorting Assistant) are Pay Level 4 posts under the Department of Posts with identical basic pay of ₹25,500. The difference is in the work location and nature. PA works at post office counters in a customer-facing role handling postal services, savings accounts, and Aadhaar-linked schemes. SA works at Mail Sorting Hubs in a backend logistics role involving mail segregation and dispatch. SA postings often involve rotational shift duties including night shifts, which PA roles typically don't. Choose based on your preference for customer interaction vs. operational work.
Financially, yes — DEO sits at Pay Level 4 or 5 versus Pay Level 2 for LDC, meaning a ₹5,600–₹9,300 higher basic pay from day one. However, DEO requires clearing an 8,000 KDPH data entry skill test, which is significantly harder than the standard typing test. If you have the typing ability, DEO (especially DEO Grade B at CAG) is the better post. If your typing speed is average, LDC gives you a stable central government role without the additional skill test risk. Assess your typing speed honestly before deciding.
You can and should fill post preferences during the application process. However, final allotment is strictly based on your All India Rank and available vacancies in each post category. Higher-ranked candidates get first pick. Simply listing PA as preference #1 doesn't guarantee a PA posting — your score determines whether that slot is available to you. This is why competitive preparation, not just clearing the cutoff, is essential if you have a specific post in mind.
JSA (Junior Secretariat Assistant) is the same as LDC in terms of pay (Pay Level 2, ₹19,900 basic) and job responsibilities. The difference is that JSA is posted in Parliament-attached offices — Lok Sabha Secretariat, Rajya Sabha Secretariat, and similar bodies. The work environment, office infrastructure, and professional exposure are generally considered superior. Most PrepGrind mentors advise preferring JSA over a general Ministry LDC posting if your rank gives you the option, purely for the better long-term working environment.
For LDC, JSA, PA, and SA, you need 35 words per minute in English or 30 words per minute in Hindi in the Typing Test. For DEO, the standard is 8,000 Key Depressions Per Hour (KDPH) in a data entry test — stricter and more accuracy-dependent. Start typing practice at least 2–3 months before your expected skill test date. For DEO specifically, daily 30–45 minute practice sessions on government-format data entry simulators are non-negotiable if you're currently below 6,000 KDPH.

Conclusion: Your Next Step

The SSC CHSL post list is not a formality to skim past — it's the career decision that sits behind all your preparation effort. PA and SA offer the strongest salary for standard typing skills. DEO, especially the CAG posting, delivers the highest pay grade but demands serious typing preparation. LDC and JSA provide broad stability with clear promotion pathways — and the difference between them is purely the prestige and environment of your workplace.

Start by assessing your typing speed honestly today, understand the vacancy trends from previous CHSL cycles, and set your post preference — then work backwards to the score you actually need to land it.
